1. Summary

Cuentero Infinito collects nothing, transmits nothing and stores nothing about you. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising. Stories are made up inside your phone and never leave it.

2. What we collect

Nothing.

We do not collect or process personal data, device identifiers, usage data, location, contacts or advertising identifiers. There is no sign-up and no login. We embed no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

3. What is stored on your phone

All of the following lives only in the app's private storage on your device. We never have access to any of it:

WhatWhereWhy
The generated storiesLocal database (SQLite)So you can hear them again
Your preferencesLocal preference storageLanguage, voice, pace, audience, length, font
The kids-mode PINLocal, stored as a SHA-256 digest, never in the clearTo stop a child leaving kids mode
The language modelThe app's files folderTo generate stories offline
The report of the last unexpected crashLocal text fileSo you can copy it and send it to us if you choose to

Android's automatic backup is switched off in this app (allowBackup="false"). None of the above is copied to Google Drive. The trade-off is that your stories do not follow you to a new phone on their own.

Everything is erased when you uninstall the app. No copy remains anywhere, because there never was a copy anywhere.

4. Network connections

The app requests the INTERNET permission for one purpose only: downloading the language model (about 731 MB) the first time you open it. That download goes to Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which — like any download on the internet — may log your IP address under its own privacy policy. We neither receive nor keep that information.

After that download, the app works offline. Nothing else is sent or received: not your stories, not what you type, not usage statistics. You can verify this by putting the phone in airplane mode.

5. Reading aloud

Stories are read aloud using the Android system's own text-to-speech engine. The story text is handed to that engine, which is a component of your device or a third-party app (usually Google's speech service).

So that the text does not leave the phone, the app offers only voices that work offline and deliberately discards any voice that requires a network connection. Even so, whatever that speech engine does with the text is governed by its developer's privacy policy.

6. Payments

The unlimited version of the app is a one-time purchase, and the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play billing:

  • We never see, process or store any payment data: no card numbers, no cardholder names, no billing addresses.
  • All the app receives from Google Play is a confirmation of whether the purchase is active.
  • Terms, refunds and the handling of your payment data are governed by Google Play's terms and privacy policy.

7. Children and kids mode

This app is meant to be downloaded, installed and set up by an adult.

"Kids mode" is a tool for that adult: it shows the story full-screen in large type and is protected by a PIN the adult chooses. It is not a product aimed at children in itself, but a feature an adult switches on under their own responsibility and supervision.

  • We collect data from nobody, children included. There is nothing a child could send us even if they tried.
  • The responsible adult decides when and under what conditions a child uses the device.
  • While kids mode is active, the app generates and displays children's stories only, and additionally runs an automatic content filter over the generated text (see the Terms of Service, "Kids mode").

If you believe a child has provided personal data through this app, write to jaiverbot77@gmail.com. The answer in advance: it is not possible, because the app has no mechanism to send data anywhere.

8. Your rights

The GDPR and equivalent laws give you rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection and portability over your personal data.

Because we process no personal data of yours, we hold nothing to give you access to or to correct. What the app stores is on your phone, under your sole control: you can erase it from the system settings ("Clear data") or by uninstalling the app.
