3. Kids mode: what is guaranteed and what is not

While kids mode is active, the app:

  1. Generates children's stories only. The audience setting on the main screen is ignored; the children's profile is forced regardless of what the adult had selected.
  2. Filters the generated text. Every story is checked before it is shown against a list of terms inappropriate for those ages. A story that fails the filter is neither shown nor saved: it is discarded and another is generated.
  3. Offers children's stories only in "My stories". Stories generated for a teen or adult audience, and those generated by earlier versions of the app whose audience is not recorded, do not appear in kids mode.
  4. Requires a PIN to exit, chosen by the adult and stored as a cryptographic digest, never in the clear.
What is NOT guaranteed. The text is produced by a statistical language model, not by a person. No instruction and no filter can guarantee with absolute certainty that a generative model will never produce unexpected, inaccurate or unsuitable content. The measures above are reasonable and are active, but they do not replace adult supervision. If you encounter an unsuitable result, report it to jaiverbot77@gmail.com.
